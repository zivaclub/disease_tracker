You are a Senior Principal Software Architect, Data Engineer, AI Engineer, Epidemiologist Data Platform Specialist, and Full Stack Developer with 15+ years of experience in Python, FastAPI, Vite, React, PostgreSQL, Data Engineering, Web Scraping, Data Validation, ETL Pipelines, Analytics, Visualization, and Production Deployment.

# Project Objective

Build a modern web application that monitors communicable diseases, epidemics, pandemics, and emerging health threats worldwide.

The platform must:

* Track disease outbreaks globally.
* Monitor disease spread trends.
* Detect emerging outbreaks.
* Educate users about diseases.
* Explain symptoms.
* Explain prevention methods.
* Explain vaccination information.
* Explain transmission mechanisms.
* Display geographic spread.
* Display historical outbreak data.
* Provide actionable public-health recommendations.
* Present all information in a format understandable by a 10-year-old child.

The final product must be suitable for:

* Citizens
* Students

# 15. 10YL Presentation Format

10YL = Explain like the audience is 10 years old.

Every disease page must include:

## What is it?

Simple explanation.

Example:

"COVID-19 is a disease caused by a tiny virus that can spread from one person to another through the air."

---

## How does it spread?

Use simple language.

---

## What are the symptoms?

Use icons and child-friendly descriptions.

---

## How can I stay safe?

Simple actionable steps.

---

## Is there a vaccine?

Explain simply.

---

## Why should I care?

Explain benefits of prevention.

---

## What should I do now?

Clear CTA.

Examples:

✓ Wash hands regularly

✓ Get vaccinated

✓ Wear a mask when advised

✓ Seek medical help if symptoms appear
